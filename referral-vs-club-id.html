Referral ID vs Club ID

Short answer: Referral IDs and club IDs are often mentioned together, but they do different jobs. Confusing them can lead to joining the wrong destination or relying on the wrong person for support.

club IDsreferral IDsPokerBrosClubGG

Club ID

A club ID typically points to a club or community destination inside the app.

Referral or agent ID

A referral ID usually identifies the person or path that introduced the player. It may be tied to onboarding or support, depending on the community.

Practical check

Ask what each ID is for, which app it applies to, and what support path exists if something is unclear.
