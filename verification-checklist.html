Verification framework

Good verification is not about being paranoid; it is about reducing avoidable confusion. Before using a club ID, confirm the destination, source, rules, and support path. If any part is unclear, wait until it is explained in writing.

Step-by-step checklist

  1. Confirm the exact app: PokerBros, ClubGG, or another platform.
  2. Confirm the club or community name associated with the ID.
  3. Ask whether a referral or agent ID is separate from the club ID.
  4. Review support expectations before joining.
  5. Look for written rules, not just screenshots or claims.
  6. Compare traffic, stakes, and formats with your actual preferences.
  7. Avoid pressure, unrealistic claims, or vague explanations.

Good vs risky signals

FAQ

What if the source cannot explain the ID?

Do not use it yet. A reliable source should be able to explain what the ID identifies and how support works.

Should I compare multiple clubs?

Yes. Comparing rules, traffic, support, and fit is better than joining the first option you see.
